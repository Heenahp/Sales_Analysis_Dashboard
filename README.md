# 📊 Sales Dashboard in Excel

## Dataset
Data used: Synthetic dataset generated to simulate real-world sales patterns across regions, categories, and customer segments — created using AI-assisted data generation for portfolio and practice purposes. No confidential/proprietary company data used.

This is an interactive Sales Dashboard that I created in Microsoft Excel to practice data analysis and dashboard design. The dashboard helps visualize sales performance using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and slicers, making it easy to explore the data from different perspectives.

## Dashboard Includes

- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Monthly Sales Trend (2024–2025)
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Interactive filters for Region, Category, and Segment

## Key Insights
- South region led in sales (~₹26–27M), followed closely by North (~₹25M); however, Technology delivered the highest profit contribution among categories despite Office Supplies and Furniture also contributing to volume — indicating profit isn't purely sales-volume driven and category-level pricing/margin strategy matters.
- Office Supplies showed the lowest profit contribution of all three categories despite steady sales, pointing to a possible high-discount or low-margin issue worth investigating.
- Corporate (₹45.77M) and Consumer (₹42.45M) segments together account for the vast majority of sales, while Home Office (₹11.32M) lags significantly — suggesting an opportunity to grow the underpenetrated Home Office segment.
- Monitor, Mouse, Printer, Laptop, and Keyboard together dominate the Top 10 products list, showing heavy dependency on core electronics/technology items rather than furniture — reinforcing the Technology category's profit lead.
- Monthly sales trend (2024–2025) shows repeated peak-and-dip cycles rather than steady growth, indicating seasonal buying patterns that could inform inventory and promotional planning.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel 2016
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Slicers (for interactive filtering by Region, Category, Segment)
